Detailed Improvements

What Changed — and Why It Matters

Each improvement was made deliberately to reduce friction, build trust, or convert more visitors into enquiries or job applicants.

01

Hero Section — Messaging That Converts

The old site opened with a heavy slider image and no visible text. Visitors had no immediate context for what PirkkaClean offers or who it serves. The proposed version opens with a sharp headline, a supporting sentence, and a CTA button — all above the fold.

Before

Slider image only. No headline, no sub-copy, no CTA visible on arrival.

After

"Luotettava siivouskumppani vaativiin yritysympäristöihin." + CTA button immediately visible.

02

Navigation — One Clean Menu with a CTA

The old site rendered its navigation menu twice — a common WordPress theme bug that adds visual noise. The proposed site has one clean menu, a persistent "Ota yhteyttä" button in the header, and an EN language toggle for non-Finnish clients.

Before

Menu rendered twice. No contact CTA in header. No language option.

After

Single clean nav. "Ota yhteyttä" always visible. EN toggle added.

03

Rekry Page — From Overwhelming to Inviting

The old rekry page presented a massive form asking for name, full address, birthdate, nationality, education level, driving licence class, shift availability, and more. Research consistently shows that long forms have high abandonment rates. The proposed version strips it back to 5 fields and a CV upload — friendly, fast, and doable on a phone.

Before

20+ field form: personal details, work history, education, availability, language skills, certifications.

After

Name, phone, email, role type, short intro + CV upload. Done in under 2 minutes.

04

Service Copy — Benefit-Led, Not Location-Led

Each service card on the old site described where PirkkaClean operates, not what the client gains. The proposed version leads with the outcome — why a clean workplace matters to the client's business — which is more persuasive and makes PirkkaClean's value immediately obvious.

Before

"Siivouspalvelua yrityksille Tampereella ja ympäristökunnissa." (Location, no benefit.)

After

"Puhtaat toimitilat luovat viihtyisyyttä ja kertovat yrityksesi arvoista." (Outcome-first.)

Performance

Page Speed — Why It Matters

The old site runs on WordPress with a full-page RevSlider plugin, jQuery, and multiple backend scripts. Every visitor triggers a PHP server to build the page from scratch. The proposed site is plain static HTML hosted on Vercel's global CDN — the files are pre-built and served instantly from the nearest server to the visitor.

Why this matters for the business: Google research shows that 53% of mobile users abandon a page that takes more than 3 seconds to load. WordPress sites with heavy plugins and sliders routinely exceed this. A static site on a CDN like Vercel loads in under 1 second, reducing bounce rates and improving Google search ranking — since page speed is a direct ranking factor for local search results.
Mobile Experience

Built for How Clients Actually Browse

Over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices. The proposed site is designed mobile-first throughout — not "responsive" as an afterthought bolted onto a desktop layout.

📐
Improved

Viewport Meta Declared

The site explicitly tells mobile browsers how to scale the layout — ensuring the page renders correctly on every screen, not zoomed out or broken.

☰
Improved

Touch-Friendly Navigation

Links and buttons are sized for fingers, not mouse clicks. The "Ota yhteyttä" CTA is prominent and easy to tap on small screens.

📋
Improved

Simplified Rekry Form on Mobile

Completing a 20+ field form on a phone is painful and leads to abandonment. The new 5-field form with CV upload is quick to complete on any device.

🖼️
Old Issue Fixed

Heavy Slider Removed

Full-width image sliders are slow and awkward on mobile. Removing RevSlider alone dramatically improves the mobile load experience.

🗺️
Added

Tappable Address → Maps

The footer address links directly to Google Maps — a small detail that removes friction when a mobile user wants to navigate to the office.

⚡
Improved

Lighter on Mobile Networks

Less code, no plugin scripts, no slider assets. The page loads faster on 4G mobile connections, keeping potential clients on the page.
