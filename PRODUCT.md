# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Finnish business and housing-company decision-makers evaluating a dependable
cleaning partner, alongside job candidates considering PirkkaClean as an
employer. Most visitors need to understand the offer, trust the company, and
reach the correct next action quickly on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present PirkkaClean as a credible, established Tampere cleaning company and
convert qualified visitors into enquiries or job applications. The static site
should be faster, clearer, and easier to maintain than the current WordPress
site while preserving the company's recognizable identity.

## Brand Personality

Honest, reliable, and warmly professional. PirkkaClean communicates with quiet
confidence and visible care rather than corporate distance or marketing hype.

## Anti-references

- The duplicated navigation, heavy slider, plugin overhead, and long recruitment
  form on the existing WordPress site.
- Generic corporate-cleaning templates with stock claims and no local character.
- Visual changes that replace the established PirkkaClean palette, butterfly
  mark, or adaptive light/dark theme.
- Decorative motion that distracts from trust, proof, or the next action.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the PirkkaClean wordmark, butterfly, blue/red/green palette, and
   established adaptive theme.
2. Put trust, proof, and a clear next action ahead of decorative effects.
3. Keep the static site fast, dependency-free, and easy to verify.
4. Design mobile-first and keep touch targets and content readable.
5. Use motion only to clarify or add restrained polish, never to gate content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard usability. Respect system color-scheme
and reduced-motion preferences. Essential information must remain available
without animation, color alone, or pointer-only interaction.
