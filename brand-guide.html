06 — Voice & Tone

Brand Voice

PirkkaClean communicates with warmth, honesty, and quiet confidence. We are experienced professionals — not a faceless corporation.

🤝

Honest

We say what we mean. No over-promises, no marketing fluff. We recommend only what the customer actually needs.

🛡️

Reliable

We speak with calm confidence built on 15+ years of experience. Steady and dependable, not flashy.

❤️

Human

We care about our staff and our clients. We write for real people, in plain language — Finnish or English.

✨

Proudly Old-Fashioned

"Hyvällä tavalla vanhanaikainen" — a little more effort for the best result. This is our core identity.

✓ Do
  • Use clear, direct language
  • Be warm but professional
  • Emphasize trust and reliability
  • Use Finnish as primary language
  • Keep copy concise and honest
✗ Don't
  • Use corporate jargon or buzzwords
  • Over-promise or exaggerate
  • Use aggressive sales language
  • Use all caps for body text
  • Alter logo colors or proportions
